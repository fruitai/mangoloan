SMS Consent

This page explains how borrowers give consent to receive Mango Loan text messages.

SMS Program

Mango Loan sends transactional SMS messages to borrowers who have agreed to receive text messages about their loan account.

  • Payment receipt confirmations
  • Payment reminder notifications
  • Loan account follow-up messages related to payments or balances

How Consent Is Collected

Borrowers provide consent verbally and/or in writing when their loan profile is created or updated. In the Mango Loan admin portal, the administrator enables the borrower text opt-in setting only after the borrower agrees to receive SMS payment receipt and payment reminder messages.

Consent is stored on the borrower record inside Mango Loan. SMS messages are sent only to borrowers with text opt-in enabled and a valid phone number on file.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on borrower activity. A borrower may receive a message when a payment is recorded, when a receipt is sent, or when a payment reminder is due. Typical volume is low, usually fewer than 10 messages per month.

Opt-Out And Help

Borrowers may reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages at any time. Borrowers may reply HELP for help.

Message and data rates may apply. SMS delivery depends on the borrower phone carrier and network availability.

Privacy

Mango Loan uses borrower phone numbers only for transactional loan-related text messages. Phone numbers and SMS consent information are not sold or shared for marketing purposes.

Mango Loan SMS messages are transactional only and are not used for marketing.
